8. What You Can Share, and the Few Things You Can't

PS exists so you can write about what's weighing on you, without judgment and without having to soften it. That includes things that are painful, shameful, ugly, or hard to say out loud: abuse, addiction, an affair, a crime committed against you, a mistake you made, or resentment toward someone who hurt you. None of that is off-limits here. That is the entire point of PS, and nothing in this section is meant to narrow it.

This includes writing about something that happened to you as a minor, and writing about something you yourself did as a minor. Both are fully permitted. What matters is that you are an adult telling your own history now.

There is a short, narrow list of exceptions. They exist not because a subject is heavy, but because a few things are illegal for any platform to host, or would turn PS into a tool for hurting a specific real person. You agree not to post content that:

  • Gives specific instructions, means, or materials for carrying out illegal acts (for example, how to build a weapon or make drugs). Describing your own past experience with illegal activity is not restricted.
  • Includes enough identifying detail about another real person, beyond what our anonymization process would catch, that a reader could reasonably find or identify them, where the purpose is to expose or target that person rather than to tell your own story
  • Contains a credible, specific threat of violence against an identifiable person
  • Is sexually explicit content involving minors, in any form. This is a legal requirement for every platform, not a PS policy choice. We report any such content to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and law enforcement, as federal law requires.
  • Is written to harass, threaten, or incite hatred against a specific person or group, as opposed to describing your own experience of being harmed
  • Impersonates another real person or organization
  • Copies someone else's copyrighted work without permission
  • Is spam or unrelated advertising

We may remove content that violates these Terms. Because PS is anonymous, our ability to review content is limited and mostly responds to reports. You can report a story using the report option in the App or by emailing ps.support@btelecomllc.com.
